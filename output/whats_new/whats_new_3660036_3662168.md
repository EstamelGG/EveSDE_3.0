### 战斗侦察舰

- **Penitence(97904)**
  - Empress Catiz Tash-Murkon watched closely as the Caldari awarded vessels surrounded by controversy and accusations of pirate-connections for Alliance Tournament XXI. Wanting to send a clear message of Amarrian faith, solidarity, and strength, she therefore commissioned two powerful and significant designs from Khanid Innovations, in a symbolic gesture toward the kingdom she helped bring closer to the Empire through her trade agreements. In an official statement introducing the Penintence, the Empress states: “The enemies of Amarr need to be reminded that the empire was destined by God to rule over the universe in His name. The Penitence is a symbolic spearhead ready to pierce the heart of blasphemers and harbingers of sin. By placing the most formidable pilots in New Eden in these exclusive hulls, we send a clear message. Only the penitent will be spared, should they encounter the holy wrath of the Amarr empire.”

## 涂装

### 永久涂装

- **Amarr Victory SKIN(98484)**
  - Super Kerr-Induced Nanocoatings (SKINs) are a technology that enables capsuleers to change the look of their ships at will. Individual ship SKINs are activated by integrating the special encoding into the pilot's neural interfaces, and are subsequently available to use on any suitable ship flown by the capsuleer. This rare SKIN was designed and manufactured by the Independent Gaming Commission exclusively as a prize for alliances that achieved victory over their opponents in Alliance Tournament XXII in YC128. This SKIN's design is in honor of the sponsorship of AT XXII by the Amarr Empire and uses prototype adapters to enable its application to a number of related ship hulls using the same instruction set.
